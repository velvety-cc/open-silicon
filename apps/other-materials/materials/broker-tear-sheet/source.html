Who we are

OpenSilicon is a dedicated financing partner for GPU infrastructure.

We provide senior secured facilities of $50m to $200m, underwritten against NVIDIA GPU assets and contracted offtake agreements. Terms run three to five years at 70-80% LTV. We assess offtakers on contract strength and payment capacity rather than credit rating alone, and our review process is built for the pace of GPU procurement.

For operators buying ahead of a long-term facility, our bridge financing covers equipment and site deployment through closing.
