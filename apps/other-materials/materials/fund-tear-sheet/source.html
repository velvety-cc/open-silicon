Fund Strategy

The fund provides senior secured loans against Nvidia GPU deployments supported by offtake contracts of three years or longer. With payback periods of approximately two years, contracted revenue retires principal within the contract term. Persistent supply constraints across current and next-generation GPUs reinforce offtaker retention and support collateral recovery values.
