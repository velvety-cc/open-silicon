Project Aurora: Capital Release Milestones

We conduct due diligence and sign off at five critical stages of Project Aurora, from pre-funding to repayment.

Before draw

T-3
  • Confirm funding sources, purchase terms and draw conditions.
  • Document security, collection rights and account control.

Loan Funding

T-2
  • Release funds against agreed purchase documents and draw conditions.
  • Route supplier payments through the agreed funding arrangements.

Delivery

T-1
  • Verify GPU identity, ownership and required security registration.
  • Link further funding releases to documented delivery milestones.

Acceptance

T+0
  • Confirm site acceptance, customer sign-off and billing conditions.
  • Reconcile customer prepayments and future billing credits.

Repayment

T+1–T+36
  • Apply controlled collections to scheduled debt service and reserves.
  • Monitor payments, equipment values and covenants.
