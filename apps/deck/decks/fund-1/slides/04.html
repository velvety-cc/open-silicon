Demand outpaces deployable supply

Demand. Enterprise AI adoption and growing inference workloads increase the compute needed to run models in production, supporting sustained demand for GPU capacity.

Supply. Power-ready sites, GPU systems and networking require substantial upfront capital and long lead times, limiting how quickly operators can add usable capacity.

Credit underwriting follows defined standards

Repayment. Offtaker credit, contracted minimum payments and controlled collections support scheduled debt service.

Recovery. Identified GPUs and first-priority security support enforcement. Conservative valuations guide expected recoveries.
