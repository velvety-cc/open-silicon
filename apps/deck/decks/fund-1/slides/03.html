01 · Investment overview

Executive Summary

AI Infra Access Fund finances NVIDIA B300 / GB300 deployments supported by 3-5-year customer contracts.

Fund strategy overview

We provide senior secured equipment and bridge financing for B300 / GB300 deployments. Matched offtake contracts across multiple customers support repayment.

Why now

AI demand is outpacing physical capacity. Equipment spending precedes customer receipts, while banks remain selective on GPU collateral.

Why this credit works

Contracted collections, identifiable GPUs and transaction-specific guarantor support. A portfolio of offtakers broadens the repayment base.
