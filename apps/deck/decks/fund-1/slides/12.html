08 · Governance and reporting

Governance, reporting and tax

Proposed investor oversight and reporting, with a Cayman fund and local project companies at operator sites.

Investor reporting and governance

  • Loan reporting. Loan reports will reconcile balances, draws and repayments, compare receipts with amounts due, and flag arrears and covenant exceptions.
  • Portfolio exposures. Portfolio reports will show offtaker, guarantor, operator, site and GPU-generation exposures, including shared dependencies.
  • Fund cash. Fund reports will disclose fees, expenses and reserves and distinguish returned principal from investment income.
  • Decision rights. Fund documents will specify approval authority, material-change consents, allocation rules and related-party conflict procedures.
  • Valuation and assurance. Fund documents will define valuation policy, audit scope and service-provider responsibilities.
