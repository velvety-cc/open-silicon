# Formula and timing documentation

All amounts are USD. The engine (`model/engine.mjs`) is deterministic, synchronous and independent of the UI, worker and any LLM. Inputs are metadata-bearing records. Templates are deeply frozen; overrides produce new immutable input versions. FNV-1a 64-bit identifies canonicalized JSON inputs and settings (a reproducibility identifier, not a cryptographic signature). The source XLSX has a separate SHA-256 hash. Every run records input/model versions, hashes and UTC calculation time.

The model has a borrower/project and this lender. Borrower cash receives sponsor equity, net lender draws, customer prepayments and collections. It pays equipment suppliers, site NRC, operating costs, reserves, workout costs it can fund and debt service. Equity is an explicit amount/date. There is no automatic sponsor or lender top-up. Equipment procurement is scheduled in the same two tranches as draws; editing draw dates changes supplier obligations as well as funding. An unpaid supplier balance remains visible and is assumed senior in recovery. A full procurement schedule independent of draw dates is a remaining extension.

## Exact daily processing

UTC calendar dates use no business-day adjustment. A day is [00:00, next 00:00). Draws and equity arrive at the beginning of their dates. Daily interest accrues after that day's receipts and payments, through the next day. A 1 Jan 2025 wire repaid on 1 Jan 2026 therefore accrues for 365 days. Dated cash flows retain wire/receipt dates, regardless of monthly chart grouping. Events with the same date follow this order:

1. Original wires net of OID, explicit equity, customer prepayment and deposit releases.
2. Equipment obligations and site NRC; pay only available cash, leave the remainder payable.
3. Default/enforcement costs. Borrower cash funds costs first, then the explicitly committed lender workout amount. Liquidation may deduct unpaid costs from sale proceeds. Restart strategies cannot restart while upfront costs are unfunded.
4. Prior-month earned revenue invoices (old and replacement buyers separately). Apply available customer prepayment credits once. Cash invoices collect on invoice date plus the selected lag and collectibility. Uncollected amounts stay in receivables; they are not presumed forgiven or collateral proceeds.
5. Earn daily compute revenue only in an operating asset state. Pay daily prorated contracted site and other operating costs; unpaid costs remain liabilities. Required reserves are separate cash balances.
6. Contractual due dates, actual interest payments, cash principal, fees, arrears catch-up and optional cash sweep.
7. On actual recovery receipt date: one ordered pool of retained cash, reserve, net equipment/supplier refund proceeds and subsequently eligible support. Costs and senior claims are unique. Surplus returns to the borrower.
8. Daily interest accrual, closing balances, liquidity gaps and reconciliation.

Customer prepayment can occur before lender wire. It is counted once as borrower cash, not equity, and never as a second receipt when later credited. The historical 30% assumption covers modeled contract revenue using 365 hours-days per year; actual monthly earned revenue uses actual calendar days, so leap-year revenue may differ. Unused prepayment is a liability and can create the explicitly selected refund claim. No refund is assumed by the illustrative zero input, not by legal inference.

## Operating cash and coverage

`racks = billed GPUs / GPUs per rack`. Spares do not create billed capacity. Equipment budget is the chosen valuation denominator, dated independently, and is not automatically replaced by BOM. Scope reconciliation must precede adding networking, installation or other capex.

`daily earned revenue = commissioned paid GPUs × capacity fraction × 24 × buyer price × max(take-or-pay fraction, utilization × ramp × availability) × (1 − credits)`. Commissioned paid GPUs equal configured quantity × the fraction of procurement capex actually paid, capped at 100%, after acceptance. A canceled second procurement tranche cannot produce full configured capacity. Supplier payments fund equipment before site NRC within the combined payable. These proportional commissioning and title assumptions require delivery/payment evidence. The contracted DC reservation remains at full specified load. Availability is a separate driver. Ramp is linear in calendar days after original billing start. Service requires acceptance. Buyer nonpayment alone stops collections, not loan acceleration or earned contract revenue. Contract termination/rights require evidence; this first version does not generate legal conclusions from the text fields.

`daily bundled DC cost = max(site monthly minimum, contracted kW × monthly DC rate) / calendar days in month`. Power is charged only once. If power is explicitly unbundled, add operating IT kW × 24 × PUE × energy price. Maintenance, network, insurance, cash taxes, other opex and reserves are independent amounts. Illustrative zero exclusions are not evidence that costs are absent. Missing numerical inputs required for a calculation invalidate the run rather than becoming zero.

`monthly CFADS = actual invoice collections − operating cash payments − reserve contributions`. Prepayment receipts, financing draws, equity and liquidation do not enter recurring CFADS. Prepayment already on hand may nevertheless fund actual debt payments. Negative CFADS before commissioning or collections remains in the result; it is not suppressed.

`recurring monthly DSCR = monthly CFADS / scheduled recurring cash interest and principal`. The app uses a monthly cash coverage view even for nonmonthly payments; it does not assert a rolling-quarter contractual covenant. Months with no scheduled service have null DSCR. Maturity is separated: `maturity coverage = max(0, maturity-month CFADS) / (maturity principal due + scheduled cash interest)`. Retained cash and collateral are visible separately; they are not recurring balloon CFADS. Covenant-specific cash definitions and testing periods require a signed agreement.

## Debt and cash principal

`face commitment = equipment valuation × LTV`; denominator/date are explicit. Two draw shares total 100% of the commitment, but undrawn funding is canceled on selected uncured borrower/site default if enabled. `original cash advanced = face draw × (1 − OID)`. Withheld OID is a fee claim, never original cash advanced. Coupon accrues on original principal + capitalized PIK + lender workout principal + remaining OID fee claim, divided by ACT/365 or ACT/360. Unpaid cash interest does not compound unless specified PIK is capitalized. PIK accrues within its selected grace period and capitalizes at cash-interest schedule dates. Capitalization is noncash and increases the total principal claim.

Repayment options are equal principal, annuity or bullet. Frequency is an integer number of months; first principal/interest dates are separate. The annuity uses nominal coupon × frequency / 12 for its payment quote; actual interest still uses selected day count. Actual day lengths/PIK/staged funding can therefore create a residual true-up at maturity. Balloon is an explicit reserved share, with all remaining funded principal also due at maturity. An amortization longer than tenor creates an additional residual balloon. Unpaid scheduled principal and interest remain arrears. Later cash can satisfy arrears; scheduled amounts do not reduce debt.

Original-first allocation pays original cash principal, then PIK, then workout principal. Pro-rata allocation pays original and PIK proportionally, then workout principal. Original recovery percentages divide original cash receipts by all original net wires actually advanced over the run, not just commitment. Milestones are recomputed against that final actually-advanced denominator and report the exact receipt date and calendar month. Fees, PIK and workout receipts do not count toward original recovery.

## Default and recovery

The selected default is assumed uncured after the entered cure period. Enforcement is cure + enforcement delay. No automatic cure success, legal opinion or buyer-to-loan cross-default is inferred. Explicitly false required rights prevent a strategy's restart/sale. Unknown rights allow only a conditional projection. Illustrative assertions of rights remain conditional until observed/contracted evidence metadata is supplied. Priority still needs substantive document review.

Asset states are exclusive: undelivered, delivered, installing, commissioning, operating, idle, preserved, relocating, replacement operating, sold. All strategies that eventually sell have explicit dated transitions: hold for the selected days then sell, restructure to extended maturity then sell, replacement operations for their selected contract then sell, or immediate liquidation after enforcement and remarketing. Sale stops future earned service revenue. Collections for already-issued invoices can still arrive afterward. Site-failure excludes operating strategies other than relocation.

`economic gross resale = equipment cost × funded fraction × max(residual floor, (1 − economic annual decay)^age) × condition`. Age is measured from first wire, never reset at relocation or restructuring. An editable fixed resale quote replaces the curve. `net proceeds = gross × (1 − forced haircut) × (1 − sale cost)`. If the quote is declared already net, neither haircut nor sale cost is deducted again. Separate legal/physical workout costs remain unique extra items. Undelivered hardware has zero sale value; only the explicit supplier refund collectibility can provide proceeds. Accounting book value uses straight-line useful life and is never used for resale.

At receipt, each pool source pays remaining unique workout costs, then specified senior claims and unpaid borrower operating/capex payables and selected customer refund claims, then lender principal under the declared allocation, unpaid cash interest and fees. Each amount is capped at its applicable claim. Cash reserve releases are not operating CFADS. Deposit released for operations is removed from deposit collateral. External sponsor deposits do not create project cash until explicitly released. Eligible guarantee/insurance/deposit receipts cover only unpaid original principal in this version and occur on the recovery receipt date. Beneficiary, availability, expiry, coverage and collectibility must all be supplied. Supports are consumed once, after collateral; unused caps remain. Other support coverage types are excluded, not silently reinterpreted.

Original principal shortfall, workout principal shortfall, unpaid interest, capitalized PIK and discounted economic loss remain separate. `net lender cash loss = −sum(all dated lender cash flows)`. It can be negative (profit). `NPV = sum(CF / (1 + target yield)^(actual days / 365))`. XIRR uses the same year basis and a disclosed numerical scan range. No roots, multiple roots or non-conventional sign changes produce a warning and no asserted unique return. No terminal mark is added to lender cash flow. An incomplete horizon reports unrecovered by horizon, not permanent loss.

## Sensitivities, search and reproducibility

Each sensitivity cell calls the same engine with immutable overrides. Threshold search samples a declared finite interval/grid, does not assume monotonicity, and reports fixed input hash, resolution, adjacent binding constraints and infeasibility. Structure search tests 24/36/48-month tenor × 0/25/50% balloon × equal-principal/annuity/bullet. Equity stays fixed unless edited. Unknown legal rights remain conditional even when numerical constraints pass; an unavailable right or unfunded restart cannot be a feasible candidate.

Exports include the exact daily ledger, monthly flows, lender dated flows, support/debt balances, original principal source buckets, scenario settings, timestamps and hashes, source/formula mapping, input metadata, warnings and any computed grids/search. Saved browser scenarios preserve immutable input/settings snapshots and a result fingerprint; duplicate and edit to create a working copy. Imported review JSON is hash/version validated before recalculation. Changing an input terminates stale worker work and discards the old result before presenting a new run.
