## Our Approach

Start with demand. Understand the deployment.

We focus on GPU deployment financing, bringing customer contracts, equipment investment and project cash flows into a single review.

Contracted compute demand helps us understand expected revenue. Equipment costs, operating cash flows and deployment milestones help us evaluate how a financing structure could fit.

Areas for discussion: new GPU purchases, cluster expansion and existing asset financing. Each opportunity is assessed individually.

Review: customers and contracts; GPU configuration, quantity, value and ownership; project cash flows; site and power readiness; deployment timing and responsibilities.

1. **Initial discussion** — Understand the project, your role, the financing need and the current state of customer demand.
2. **Project review** — Review contracts, equipment, cash flows and deployment readiness to assess financing fit.
3. **Financing structure** — Where the review supports proceeding, discuss a structure and terms suited to the project.
4. **Documentation and deployment** — For a project that proceeds, agree documentation, responsibilities and conditions for deployment.

An initial discussion or review does not guarantee approval, financing or a deployment date.
